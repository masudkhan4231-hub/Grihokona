Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Usually 1–2 days inside Dhaka and 3–5 days outside Dhaka, once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Do I need to pay in advance?

No. We only offer Cash on Delivery — you pay the courier when your parcel arrives.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery if there's a genuine issue with your order, and we'll sort it out.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, fill in your delivery details at checkout, and confirm — it opens WhatsApp with your order ready to send.

What if a product is out of stock?

Tap "Notify Me" on the product — it opens WhatsApp so we can message you the moment it's back.
